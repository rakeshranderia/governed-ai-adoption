# How to Use This Library

Use it as an executive reference, a program starter and an evidence model.

## Minimum viable sequence
1. Define approved AI platforms.
2. Establish acceptable-use and data-handling guardrails.
3. Identify pilot users.
4. Select practical use cases.
5. Train the cohort.
6. Track usage and outcomes.
7. Gather feedback and incidents.
8. Scale successful use cases.
9. Retire or restrict uses that do not justify risk or cost.

## Evidence to retain
Pilot charter, approved-use guidance, training record, use-case register, AI risk assessments, adoption telemetry, survey feedback, incident/exception log, benefits register and scale/stop decisions.
