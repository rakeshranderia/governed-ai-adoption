# Metrics and Reporting

Combine **adoption, value, risk and cost**.

## Adoption
Licensed users, active users, repeat use, frequency, adoption by team.

## Value
High-value use cases, estimated time saved, cycle-time improvement, quality improvement, avoided manual effort.

## Risk
Incidents, exceptions, Shadow AI, data-handling issues, overdue reviews.

## Enablement
Training completion, confidence, support demand.

## Cost
Licence utilisation and cost per active user.

Avoid presenting licence count as adoption. A better story is:

**Access → Active Use → Repeat Use → Valuable Use → Controlled Scale**
